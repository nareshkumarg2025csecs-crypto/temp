Fire & Explosion Response

Introduction

Welcome to the Fire & Explosion Response training module.

Fire and explosions are among the most serious hazards in mining and industrial workplaces. A small ignition source, flammable material, or unsafe condition can quickly develop into an emergency.

In this module, you will learn how to recognize fire hazards, identify emergency exits, understand fire-extinguisher selection, and follow the correct evacuation sequence.

The purpose of this training is not to create fear, but to help you stay calm, recognize danger early, and follow the correct safety procedure during an emergency.

What Will You Learn?

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

Identify common fire and explosion hazards in an industrial environment.
Recognize emergency exits and safe evacuation routes.
Identify appropriate fire-response equipment for a given situation.
Understand the correct sequence of actions during a fire emergency.
Identify the designated assembly or safe area.
Make appropriate safety decisions during simulated emergency scenarios.

Why Is This Important?

During an emergency, every second matters.

Workers may panic or make incorrect decisions when they encounter an unexpected fire. Knowing the correct procedure in advance helps reduce confusion and allows workers to respond more effectively.

Traditional training can explain these procedures through manuals, lectures, and demonstrations. In this course, you will also practice these situations using Augmented Reality (AR).

You will see simulated hazards in your surroundings through your smartphone camera and interact with them as part of the training.

What Will You Do in This Course?

Your learning journey will follow four stages:

Learn

First, you will study the basic concepts of fire safety through text, images, audio, and animations.

Practice

Next, you will enter an AR simulation where you will identify hazards, locate emergency exits, and interact with simulated safety equipment.

Respond

You will then face simulated emergency situations and make decisions about what action should be taken.

Assess

Finally, you will complete an assessment. Your score will determine whether you have successfully completed the module.

How AR Will Be Used

Augmented Reality combines the real environment seen through your phone camera with digital safety information and simulated objects.

For example, during this module you may see a simulated fire placed within your surroundings:

Real Environment + AR Fire + Emergency Exit + Safety Equipment

You may then be asked to:

• Identify the fire hazard.
• Locate the safest exit.
• Select the appropriate response equipment.
• Follow the indicated evacuation route.

Your interactions will be recorded as part of the training assessment.

Important Safety Principle

Remember:

Your safety comes first.

The AR scenarios in this course are simulations. They are designed to help you understand safety procedures without exposing you to an actual fire or hazardous environment.

Always follow the site-specific emergency procedures, instructions from authorized personnel, and applicable safety rules at your workplace.

Course Completion

After completing the learning and AR activities, you will take a final assessment.

If you achieve the required passing score:

Training Completed → Assessment Passed → Digital Certificate Issued

Your certificate will contain a unique identification number and QR code for verification.

If you do not achieve the required score, you will be given an opportunity to review the relevant training content and repeat the assessment.
